👨‍👩‍👧 Made for families

Grown-ups stay in control

Mindo Kids is designed to be safe, calm and screen-time friendly.

  • Up to 5 child profiles. Each child gets their own avatar, progress, stars and badges.
  • Daily screen-time limit. Set minutes per child in the Parent Zone; the app gently stops when time is up.
  • Parent gate. Settings, purchases and links are locked behind a grown-up check.
  • Progress at a glance. See lessons completed, stars earned and streaks for every child.
  • No ads, no chat. No advertising, no public profiles and no social features for children.
  • Works offline. Lessons opened before stay on the device; progress syncs when you're back online.

💬 Questions

Frequently asked questions

What age is Mindo Kids for?

Mindo Kids is made for children aged about 1.5 to 6. Lessons are short (around 3 minutes) and every letter, word and question is read aloud, so children who can't read yet can still play.

Which languages can my child learn?

English, Bangla, Arabic, Hindi and Urdu — each with its full alphabet, vowel signs and everyday words — plus a Math World for numbers, counting and shapes. Arabic and Urdu lessons display right to left.

Is it really free?

Yes — you can download Mindo Kids and play many beginner lessons for free, with no ads. Mindo Plus is an optional subscription that unlocks every lesson in every world.

How do I add another child or set screen time?

Open Profile → Parent Zone. There you can add up to 5 children and set a daily screen-time limit for each child.

I paid but lessons are still locked — what do I do?

Open the subscription screen and tap “Restore purchases” while signed in with the same Google Play account you used to subscribe.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Lessons you have opened before stay on the device, and progress syncs automatically when you are back online.

What data do you collect about my child?

Only a first name or nickname, an avatar, an age band and learning progress. No photos, birth dates or location. Read our Privacy Policy for details. You can delete a child profile or your whole account at any time in the Parent Zone.
