Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Mindo Kids (“we”, “the app”) is a learning app for young children. Accounts are created and managed by a parent or guardian. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Parent account: name and e-mail address, used to sign in and keep your family’s data in sync.
  • Child profiles: a first name or nickname, a chosen avatar and an age band. We do not collect photos, birth dates, voice recordings or location.
  • Learning progress: lessons completed, stars, badges, streaks and screen-time settings.
  • Subscription status: whether your family has an active Mindo Plus subscription, as confirmed by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: show the right lessons, save progress and award badges.
  • To let parents follow each child’s progress and set screen time.
  • To unlock premium lessons for subscribers.

What we never do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • We do not sell or rent any personal data.
  • No chat, public profiles or social features for children.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (authentication, database and hosting) to store account and progress data securely, and Google Play Billing to process subscriptions. This website uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count anonymous visits and download-button taps; Google Signals and ad personalisation are turned off. Letters and words are read aloud using the text-to-speech engine on your own device.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete a child profile, or your whole account and all associated data, at any time in the app under Profile → Parent Zone. Subscriptions must be cancelled separately in Google Play.

Children’s privacy

The app is designed for children under the supervision of a parent. Purchases, settings and external links are protected by a parent gate. We only collect the minimum information needed to run the app.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions? Contact us through the developer e-mail address shown on our Google Play page.
